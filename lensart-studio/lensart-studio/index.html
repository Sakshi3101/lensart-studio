About LensArt

The Artists
Behind the Lens

"Photography is not about the camera — it's about the connection between the photographer and the moment."

Founded in 2012, LensArt Studio has grown from a one-person passion project into an award-winning team of 8 visual storytellers. Based in Mumbai with coverage across India, we specialize in luxury wedding photography, vibrant festival shoots, and professional corporate events.

We use Sony Alpha & Canon EOS R systems paired with premium prime lenses to ensure every image is technically flawless and emotionally resonant. Our post-processing pipeline delivers consistent, cinematic results.
